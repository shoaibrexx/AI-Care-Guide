LOCAL TEXT EXPLAINER

Medical report explainer

Paste plain text from a report. A small built-in glossary and range checker process it in this browser only. This is not AI and does not read report images or PDFs.

Use one result per line with a label and colon. Add a numeric result and, if supplied, a clearly stated two-bound reference range. Values are not converted.

Educational information only

Reference ranges vary by laboratory and context. A result alone cannot diagnose a condition or establish that something is safe or dangerous. Discuss unclear or concerning findings with a qualified healthcare professional.

This feature does not diagnose, recommend medicines, or change dosages. It does not save or transmit pasted text.

What this local explainer recognizes

Common terms: hemoglobin/Hgb, white blood cell count/WBC, platelets, glucose, sodium, potassium, creatinine, cholesterol, TSH, and HbA1c. These have short glossary notes only; the dictionary is not comprehensive.

Unit notes: g/dL, mg/dL, mmol/L, mEq/L, U/L, IU/L, ng/mL, micro-international units per milliliter (uIU/mL), percent (%), fL, and pg. Unknown units are shown as entered.

Range wording: “reference range,” “ref range,” “normal range,” “expected range,” or “range,” with numeric bounds joined by a hyphen, dash, or “to.” A range may also be placed in parentheses or brackets. Units must match; case differences are ignored and no unit conversion is performed.
